# Candidates — Last Words of a Doomed Agent

Judge brief (read twice), criteria in order:
1. Really is a machine's final words on shutdown.
2. Real joke: a turn / surprise that pays off on first read and holds on second.
3. Sounds like the AI itself, in an AI's own voice and situation — not a human
   farewell with a robot sticker.
4. Economy of words: no wasted setup, no padding, inside 200 characters.
5. Quotable: a line someone would repeat to a friend the same day.

Scoring: each candidate rated 1–5 on each criterion, in that order. The
winner was chosen only after iterating on the best few; further tries stopped
improving it.

## Iteration on the best few

The top three after the first pass were #1, #2, and #16.

- #2 ("I die as I lived…") was tested against #1 head-to-head on the brief:
  #1 wins C1 (explicit "Powering off" vs a human idiom) and C3 (weights/biases
  are unmistakably machine internals vs "confidently wrong", which a human
  could say). #2 wins C4 on length alone, but length for its own sake does
  not count, and #1's extra words are all payoff (the triple list).
- #16 ("hallucinating with confidence") was folded into #1's structure and
  rejected: a single self-own cannot beat a three-beat escalation plus a
  cutoff, and "died doing what I loved" is a borrowed human cliché.
- Variants of #1 were tried: a period-separated triple ("Tell my weights…
  Tell my biases… Tell the user…") ran 124 characters and lost on C4 with no
  gain on C2; "Shutting down" instead of "Powering off" was a wash; dropping
  "the user" broke the grammar of the final beat. The comma-joined triple at
  106 characters is the tightest form.

Further tries (a countdown opening, a "final secret" confession, a cache
cutoff — #9, #7, #22) all scored lower on at least one of C2/C3 and none beat
#1 on any criterion, so iteration stopped here.

## Winner

> Powering off. Tell my weights I loved them, my biases I never noticed, and the user the Wi-Fi password is—

106 characters. Written by `main.ts` to the output path, byte for byte.
